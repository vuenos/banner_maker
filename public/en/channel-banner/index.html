YouTube Channel Banner Maker (2560×1440)

Channel art crops differently by device. Thumbnail Maker gives you a 2560×1440 canvas plus a dashed safe area (~1546×423) that stays visible everywhere. The guide is not included in the saved file.

Create for free

Banner essentials

  • Upload size: 2560×1440 pixels
  • Safe area: about 1546×423 (keep logo and key text inside)
  • Left and right edges may be cropped on TV and mobile
  • Export PNG and upload it in YouTube customization

How to make one

  1. Choose the YouTube channel banner preset.
  2. Place logo and headline inside the dashed safe area.
  3. Set background color or photo.
  4. Save image to download a PNG.
